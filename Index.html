04Every module

All of Neopia, covered.

Flip on what helps, leave off what doesn't. Every module runs in isolation, so one broken page never drags the rest of the site down with it.

🎨
Sitewide Chrome
The backbone of NeoUI — injects the persistent topbar, global styles, and the settings drawer across every Neopets page.
🔍
Item Search Buttons
Adds one-click search buttons within item images sitewide, firing the Shop Wizard or SSW without leaving your current page.
🏠
Home Dashboard
Transforms the Neopets homepage into a personal hub — greeting, active timer pills, and quick links to your most-used pages.
⚡
Quickref
Reskins the Quick Reference page with pet cards showing stats, species, and active training side-by-side.
📬
Neomail
Redesigns the inbox as a fast single-page app with tabbed folders, username search, message counts, and conversation labels.
💬
Neoboards
Cleans up the boards with better thread density, readable fonts, and a posting toolbar that stays in reach on mobile.
🏰
Guilds
Reskins the guild hub and notice board with your active theme — headers, tables, and post layouts all get the treatment.
🤝
Neofriends
Modernizes the friends list and online indicator with a card layout and cleaner status display.
👤
Profiles & Lookups
Applies theme-aware styles to user lookups and pet pages, making them consistent with the rest of your NeoUI skin, all without making the original unviewable
📰
Neopian Times
Cleans up the NT with readable article typography, better image handling, and a layout that actually works on mobile.
📢
New Features
Reskins the New Features feed with a themed topbar and quicklinks, so site announcements read like the rest of NeoUI.
🗺️
Explore Neopia
Reskins the world map and location pages — backgrounds, headers, and navigation all brought into your active theme.
🌋
Training Schools
Replaces the native stat table with responsive pet cards showing level, stats, active course, and a live countdown timer.
⚔️
Battledome
Overhauled battle UI with clearer ability selection, HP readouts, and a layout that fits on a phone screen without pinching.
💰
Economy Suite
Cleaner bank, stock, and trade tables with color-coded gain/loss, your portfolio total, and sortable columns.
🧙
Shop Wizard
Results combine into a master list as you complete new searches for the same item, allowing you to be more efficient with your shop wizard use.
🎒
Inventory
A grid-based inventory view with item icons, stack counts, and one-tap actions — no more endlessly scrolling a flat list.
👀
Quest Watcher
Watches for a random faerie quest in the background and pings you the moment one shows up, keeping you reminded of what was asked for. 
⏱️
All Events
The daily hub — every timed activity in one panel with individual countdowns, ready indicators, and one-tap navigation.
🐱
Kadoatery
A dense grid showing every Kadoatie with fed/hungry status, who fed them, and tap-to-copy item name for speed feeding.
🎮
Games Room
The games directory restyled with better category navigation and game cards that actually show scores and rankings.
